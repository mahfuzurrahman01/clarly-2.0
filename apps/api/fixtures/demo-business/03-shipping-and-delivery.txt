Shipping and Delivery

Inside Dhaka: delivery in 1 to 2 business days. Delivery charge 80 BDT. Free delivery on orders above 5,000 BDT.
Outside Dhaka: delivery in 3 to 5 business days. Delivery charge 150 BDT. Free delivery on orders above 10,000 BDT.
Same-day delivery is available inside Dhaka for orders confirmed before 12:00 PM, for an extra charge of 250 BDT.

We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh through Pathao Courier and Sundarban Courier. We do not currently ship outside Bangladesh.

Cash on delivery is available nationwide for orders up to 50,000 BDT. Orders above 50,000 BDT require advance payment or a 20 percent deposit.

Order tracking: a tracking link is sent by WhatsApp as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse. If you have not received a tracking link within 48 hours of ordering, please share your order number and we will check it.

Failed delivery: our courier attempts delivery twice. After two failed attempts the parcel returns to our warehouse and we will contact you to arrange redelivery. A second delivery charge applies.
