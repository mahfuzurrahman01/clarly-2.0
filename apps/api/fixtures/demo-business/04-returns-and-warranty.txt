Returns, Refunds and Warranty

Returns
You may return most items within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, undamaged, and in its original sealed packaging with all accessories and the invoice. Return delivery is paid by the customer unless the item is defective or we sent the wrong item.

Items that cannot be returned: earphones and in-ear headphones once the seal is broken (hygiene), software and digital licences, and any item with physical damage caused after delivery.

Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bKash or bank transfer.

Exchanges
Exchanges are available within 7 days for a different colour or model. Any price difference must be settled at the time of exchange.

Warranty
- Smartphones and laptops: 1 year official brand warranty, handled through the brand's authorised service centre.
- Accessories and chargers: 6 months replacement warranty from Bolt Electronics.
- Headphones: 1 year warranty, battery excluded.

Warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repair, or normal battery wear. Please keep your invoice; warranty claims cannot be processed without proof of purchase.

Dead on arrival: if a product does not power on when you first receive it, contact us within 48 hours and we will replace the unit at no cost.
