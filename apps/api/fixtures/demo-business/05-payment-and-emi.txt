Payment Methods and EMI

We accept:
- Cash on delivery, up to 50,000 BDT
- bKash, Nagad and Rocket mobile payments
- Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- Bank transfer to our City Bank account (details provided on request)

EMI (installment plans)
0 percent EMI is available on purchases above 15,000 BDT with credit cards from City Bank, BRAC Bank, Eastern Bank, Dutch-Bangla Bank and Standard Chartered.
Available tenures: 3, 6, 9 and 12 months. Tenures of 18 and 24 months are available on purchases above 100,000 BDT.
EMI is only available on credit cards, not debit cards. The bank may charge a processing fee, which is set by the bank and not by Bolt Electronics.

Invoices
A VAT invoice is issued with every order and included in the parcel. If you need a duplicate invoice or a corporate invoice with your company name and BIN, share your order number and company details and we will email it within one business day.
