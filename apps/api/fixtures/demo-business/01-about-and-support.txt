About Bolt Electronics

Bolt Electronics is a consumer electronics retailer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We have been selling laptops, smartphones, headphones, and accessories since 2019. We operate one physical showroom in Dhanmondi and ship nationwide.

What our WhatsApp support assistant can help you with:
- Product availability, specifications, and current prices
- Order status and delivery tracking
- Shipping options, delivery times, and delivery charges
- Returns, refunds, exchanges, and warranty claims
- Payment methods, EMI plans, and invoices
- Showroom address, opening hours, and how to reach a human agent

If you simply say hello, we are happy to help — just tell us what you need, such as "where is my order", "do you have the Galaxy A55", or "how do I return an item". If your question is outside these topics, or you would prefer to speak with a person, say "talk to an agent" and we will connect you with our support team.
