Shipping and Returns Policy

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra 200 taka.
We ship to Canada and the United States; international delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.
Opened items can be returned only if the product is defective.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the return.

Order tracking links are sent by WhatsApp as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse.
