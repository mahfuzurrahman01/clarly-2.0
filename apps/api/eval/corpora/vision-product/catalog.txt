--- Warranty claims ---
A warranty claim needs the original receipt and the product's serial number.
Claims are processed at the branch of purchase within 10 working days.
Physical damage, water damage and burnt motors caused by overloading are not covered.

--- Damaged on arrival ---
A product that arrives damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
Report it with a photo of the damage and the order number; a replacement is dispatched at no cost.
