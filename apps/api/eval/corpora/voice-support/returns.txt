--- Returns and refunds ---

General return window: 7 days from delivery for any unused item in its original packaging.
Eid sale purchases: 30 days from delivery.
Opened earphones and other hygiene-sealed items cannot be returned once the seal is broken, unless the item is defective.
Defective items can always be returned, opened or not.

Return shipping is paid by the customer, except for defective or wrongly-shipped items.
There is no restocking fee.

Refund timing: 5 to 7 working days after the returned item is received and checked.
Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bKash number the customer provides.

--- Delivery ---
Inside Dhaka: 2 working days. Outside Dhaka: 3 to 5 working days.
Delivery charge inside Dhaka is 60 BDT and outside Dhaka is 120 BDT.
