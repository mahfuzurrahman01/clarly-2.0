Returns and Refunds Policy

General returns: most items may be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Extended returns: items bought during a Eid or New Year promotional campaign may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Hygiene exception: earphones, in-ear headphones and personal grooming devices cannot be returned once the seal is broken, regardless of the 7-day or 30-day window.
Defective items: a defective item may be returned within 30 days regardless of category, including sealed-broken hygiene items.

Refund timing: refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item.
Refund method: refunds go to the original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bKash or bank transfer.
Return shipping: paid by the customer, unless the item is defective or we shipped the wrong item, in which case we pay.
Restocking fee: there is no restocking fee.
