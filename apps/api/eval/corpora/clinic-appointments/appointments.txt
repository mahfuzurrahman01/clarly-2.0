Shefa Diagnostic Clinic - Appointments, Payment and Insurance

Appointments: book by phone on 09612-000000 or at the front desk. Cardiology and dermatology require an appointment; general physician consultation does not.
Rescheduling: an appointment can be rescheduled up to 4 hours before the slot at no charge. A missed appointment forfeits the booking fee of 200 BDT.
Payment: cash, bKash and cards are accepted at the front desk. Payment is taken at the time of service.
Insurance: we accept direct billing from MetLife and Green Delta only. For any other insurer, pay at the desk and claim with our receipt.
Corporate accounts: employees of registered corporate clients are billed to the company; ask the front desk to confirm your company is registered.
Reports: same-day tests are ready after 6:00 PM. Reports are collected from the front desk or emailed on request.
