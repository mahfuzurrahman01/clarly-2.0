Nokkhotro Catering and Events - Dietary Information

All packages are fully halal. We do not serve pork or alcohol.
Vegetarian substitution is available for any package at no extra cost; tell us the number of vegetarian guests at least 7 days before the event.

Allergens: our kitchen handles peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, eggs, mustard, sesame and shellfish. Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen.
We cannot guarantee any dish is free of cross-contamination.
Guests with a diagnosed food allergy must be discussed with our operations manager before the event is confirmed. Front-desk and chat staff are not authorised to confirm that any dish is safe for an allergic guest.

Children's portions are charged at full price. There is no separate children's menu.
