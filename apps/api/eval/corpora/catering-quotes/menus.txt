Nokkhotro Catering and Events - Menus and Pricing (per person, minimum 40 guests)

Silver package - 950 BDT per person - 5 items: plain rice, chicken roast, vegetable, dal, dessert.
Gold package - 1,450 BDT per person - 8 items: kacchi biryani, beef rezala, chicken roast, borhani, salad, dessert, soft drink, mineral water.
Platinum package - 2,100 BDT per person - 12 items: kacchi biryani, mutton rezala, prawn, grilled chicken, borhani, three salads, two desserts, soft drink, mineral water.

Surcharges
Guest count 40 to 99: standard package price.
Guest count 100 to 249: 5 percent discount on the package price.
Guest count 250 and above: 8 percent discount on the package price.
Friday and Saturday events: 10 percent surcharge on the package price, applied before any guest-count discount.
Events outside Dhaka city: 15,000 BDT transport charge, flat, regardless of guest count.
Live cooking station: 25,000 BDT flat, available only for Gold and Platinum.

Service staff: one waiter per 25 guests is included. Extra waiters are 1,200 BDT each.
