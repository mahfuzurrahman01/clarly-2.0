Orders, Delivery and Returns

Inside Dhaka: delivery in 1 to 2 business days. Outside Dhaka: 3 to 5 business days.
A delayed order can be tracked with the tracking link sent by SMS at dispatch.
Returns: items may be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Defective items: a defective item may be returned within 30 days and is replaced or refunded in full.
Refund timing: refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item.
Damaged in transit: report within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we replace the item at no cost.
