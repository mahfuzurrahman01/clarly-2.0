Payments

Payment methods accepted: bKash, Nagad, Visa and Mastercard cards, and bank transfer.
Card payments are taken on our secure checkout page only. We never take card details over chat or phone.
Cash on delivery is available inside Dhaka only.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
An order can be looked up with the order number, which starts with BLT- and is in the confirmation SMS.
