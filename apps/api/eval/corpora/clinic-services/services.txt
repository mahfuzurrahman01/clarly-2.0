Shefa Diagnostic Clinic - Services and Fees

Consultations
General physician consultation - 800 BDT - no appointment needed, first come first served.
Cardiology consultation - 1,500 BDT - appointment required.
Dermatology consultation - 1,200 BDT - appointment required.

Diagnostics
Complete blood count (CBC) - 450 BDT - report same day.
Lipid profile - 1,100 BDT - requires 12 hours fasting - report same day.
Thyroid panel (TSH, T3, T4) - 1,600 BDT - report next day.
Chest X-ray - 900 BDT - report within 3 hours.
Ultrasound of whole abdomen - 2,200 BDT - requires 6 hours fasting and a full bladder - report same day.

Reports are collected from the front desk or sent by email on request.
