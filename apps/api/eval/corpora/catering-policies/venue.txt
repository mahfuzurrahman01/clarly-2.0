Nokkhotro Catering and Events - Venue and Operations

We cater at the customer's venue. We do not own or rent venues, and we do not book venues on a customer's behalf.
Setup: our team arrives 3 hours before the serving time. The venue must provide access, water and a power point by then.
Serving window: a package covers 3 hours of service. Each additional hour is 8,000 BDT.
Late-night events: serving after 11:00 PM requires the venue's own permission and is subject to a 20 percent late-night surcharge.

Cutlery, plates and chafing dishes are included. Tables, chairs, decor and lighting are not included and we do not supply them.
Leftover food remains the property of the customer and must be removed at the end of the event; we do not store or redistribute it.

Complaints about food quality must be raised in writing within 24 hours of the event.
