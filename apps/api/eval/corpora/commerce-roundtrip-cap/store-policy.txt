Store Hours, Delivery and Returns Policy

Store Hours:
Our support team is available Saturday to Thursday from 9 in the morning until 10 at night, and on Friday from 2 in the afternoon until 10 at night, Gulf Standard Time. We are closed during Eid and other public holidays; replies may be delayed over those periods.

Delivery Zones and Timeframes:
We currently deliver to addresses across Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. Orders to major cities such as Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah typically arrive within two to four business days of dispatch. Orders to other cities and remote areas may take a few extra business days. Delivery windows can shift during peak seasons or bad weather — once a shipment has been dispatched, the courier's tracking status is always the most accurate source for arrival timing. The delivery fee, if any, is shown at checkout before the order is confirmed and depends on the destination and order weight; we never charge it separately after confirmation.

Returns and Refunds:
Most items can be returned within seven days of delivery if unused, in original packaging, and with all accessories included. Personal care and hygiene items cannot be returned once opened. Defective items or items that do not match what was ordered can be returned regardless of the usual window, and in that case we cover the return shipping; otherwise return shipping is the customer's responsibility. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method once the returned item is received and inspected. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer instead. There is no restocking fee.
